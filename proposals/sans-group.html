The four pillars of the monthly work

每月工作的四大支柱

What each term means, and what it is for. Web maintenance and content updates run underneath all four.

每个名词的意思，以及它的目的。网站维护与内容更新贯穿这四项。

  • SEO Search Engine Optimisation搜索引擎优化 Making pages rank in Google's ordinary results: technical health, page speed, site structure, keywords and internal links.让网页在 Google 一般搜索结果中排名：技术健康、页面速度、网站结构、关键词与内部链接。 Goal: Be found when people search for what Sans offers.目标：当人们搜索 Sans 提供的服务时，找得到你。
  • AEO Answer Engine Optimisation答案引擎优化 Shaping content so it can be lifted as a direct answer: Google's featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice assistants. Clear questions, clear answers, FAQ markup.把内容整理成可以直接被引用为答案的形式：Google 精选摘要、“其他人还问了”与语音助手。清楚的问题、清楚的答案、FAQ 结构化数据。 Goal: Be the answer that is shown, not just one link among ten.目标：成为被展示的答案，而不只是十个链接之一。
  • GEO Generative Engine Optimisation生成式引擎优化 Making the brand understood and cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews: clear facts, consistent brand details, authoritative content.让 ChatGPT、Gemini、Perplexity 与 Google AI 概览等 AI 助手正确理解并引用品牌：清晰的事实、一致的品牌信息、有权威的内容。 Goal: Be the name that comes up when people ask an AI.目标：当人们询问 AI 时，被提到的是你。
  • CRO Conversion Rate Optimisation转化率优化 Improving the pages so more visitors take the next step: an enquiry, a WhatsApp message, a booking. Clearer calls to action, simpler forms, better layouts.改进页面，让更多访客采取下一步：询问、发 WhatsApp、预约。更清楚的行动按钮、更简单的表单、更好的版面。 Goal: Turn visits into customers.目标：把访问变成顾客。

  3. The website fee covers a full rebuild of sansgroup.com.my: migration of the entire site, the rebuild, and the first month of SEO work. A second site, such as sanswellness.com, would be quoted separately.
  4. 网站费用涵盖 sansgroup.com.my 的完整重建：整站迁移、网站重建，以及第一个月的 SEO 工作。第二个网站（例如 sanswellness.com）另行报价。

  8. 我们不会在这里承诺：不保证排名、不承诺流量数字、不承诺百分比提升。我们不公布自己站不住的数字；会谈中引用的任何数据，都会说明它出自哪个客户。
  9. This proposal is valid for 30 days from the date on the cover. Scope, sequence and payment schedule are settled in writing before work starts.
  10. 本方案自封面日期起 30 天内有效。具体范围、顺序与付款安排，在开工前以书面确认。
